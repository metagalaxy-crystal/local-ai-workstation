Let \( c \) be the number of chickens and \( r \) be the number of rabbits.

From the problem, we know:
- The total number of heads is 35, so \( c + r = 35 \).
- The total number of legs is 94, so \( 2c + 4r = 94 \) (since chickens have 2 legs and rabbits have 4 legs).

Now solve the system of equations.

From the first equation, express \( c \) in terms of \( r \):
\[ c = 35 - r \]

Substitute into the second equation:
\[
2(35 - r) + 4r = 94
\]
Simplify:
\[
70 - 2r + 4r = 94
\]
\[
70 + 2r = 94
\]
Subtract 70 from both sides:
\[
2r = 24
\]
Divide by 2:
\[
r = 12
\]

Now substitute \( r = 12 \) back into \( c = 35 - r \):
\[
c = 35 - 12 = 23
\]

Thus, there are \(\boxed{23}\) chickens and \(\boxed{12}\) rabbits.
