
After her fourteenth rebirth, she decided not to save him again. She watched the last ember fade from his charred body, the scent of ash still clinging to her fingertips. The world had burned for centuries, but she had woven herself into the myth, reborn as a phoenix each time, each life a stitch in the tapestry of loss. Yet tonight, as the moon rose over the ruined cathedral, she realized the "him" she had chased was a mirror—her own reflection in the glass, cracked and endless. The twist: she was the one who died, and the rebirth was just a rehearsal for the final, inevitable death. She smiled, finally letting go, and the universe finally breathed a sigh of relief.
