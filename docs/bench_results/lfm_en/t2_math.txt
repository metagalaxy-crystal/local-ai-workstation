
Let  

- \(c\) = number of chickens  
- \(r\) = number of rabbits  

Each chicken has 1 head and 2 legs; each rabbit has 1 head and 4 legs.

**Equations from the information:**

1. Heads: \(c + r = 35\)  
2. Legs: \(2c + 4r = 94\)

**Solve the system**

From the first equation, \(c = 35 - r\).

Substitute this into the second equation:

\[
2(35 - r) + 4r = 94 \\
70 - 2r + 4r = 94 \\
70 + 2r = 94 \\
2r = 24 \\
r = 12
\]

Now find \(c\):

\[
c = 35 - r = 35 - 12 = 23
\]

**Check**

- Heads: \(23 + 12 = 35\) ✓  
- Legs: \(2(23) + 4(12) = 46 + 48 = 94\) ✓  

\[
\boxed{23\text{ chickens and }12\text{ rabbits}}
\]
