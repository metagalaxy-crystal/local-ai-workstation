- Let **$R$** = number of rabbits

$$
C + R = 35 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
$$

#### **Equation 2: Legs**
- Chickens have **2 legs** each → $2C$ legs  
- Rabbits have **4 legs** each → $4R$ legs  
Total legs = $2C + 4R = 94$:
$$
2C + 4R = 94 \quad \text{(Equation 2)}
$$

---

### **Step 3: Solve the System**

$$
R = 35 - C
$$

$$
2C + 4(35 - C) = 94
$$
$$

$$

$$

C = 23
