The novel **'Pride and Prejudice'** was written by **Jane Austen**.

It was first published in the **19th century**. Specifically, it was published on **July 28, 1813**, placing it firmly within the 19th century (the years 1801–1900).

**Key Details:**
- **Author:** Jane Austen (1775–1817)
- **Publication Year:** 1813
- **Century:** 19th Century
- **Significance:** This novel is widely regarded as one of the foundational works of English literature and a masterful exploration of themes like pride, prejudice, social class, and marriage in Regency-era England.
