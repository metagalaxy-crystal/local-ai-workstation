Thinking Process:

1.  **Analyze the Request:**
    *   Format: Micro-fiction.
    *   Length: No more than 150 words.
    *   Opening Sentence: "After her fourteenth rebirth, she decided not to save him again."
    *   Requirement: Include an unexpected twist.
    *   Output: Only the story.

2.  **Drafting - Sentence 1:**
    After her fourteenth rebirth, she decided not to save him again.

3.  **Brainstorming the Twist:**
    *   Idea 1: She saves him, but he doesn't notice. (Too mundane)
    *   Idea 2: He is actually the one who died. (Simple, effective)
    *   Idea 3: He is not a person, but a concept/place. (A bit complex)
    *   Idea 4: She dies immediately after. (Short)
    *   Idea 5: The twist involves who "she" is.

    Let's go with a mix of simplicity and impact.

    *   *Scenario:* She turns away. He walks past her.
    *   *Twist:* He isn't a reincarnated hero or lover, but something else entirely, or the perspective shifts.
