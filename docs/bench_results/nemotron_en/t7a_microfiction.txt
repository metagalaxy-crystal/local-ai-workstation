After her fourteenth rebirth, she decided not to save him again. The same doomed alley, the same desperate plea, the same crumbling building—she walked past. He screamed, "Please, save me!" for the fifteenth time. This time, she smiled faintly. "You were never in danger," she whispered, turning away. As she vanished into the crowd, a pigeon dove, snatching his last, futile gasp. The truth: she hadn't been his savior, but his kidnapper, resetting his trauma loop each life. His "rescue" was the cage; her refusal broke it. She finally freed him, not from death, but from her.
