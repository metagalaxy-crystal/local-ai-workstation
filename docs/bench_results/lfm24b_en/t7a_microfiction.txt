After her fourteenth rebirth, she decided not to save him again. Each time, his death haunted her, a relentless cycle of grief. She’d learned to brace herself, to watch from afar. But this time, a stranger appeared at the crossroads—a child, trembling in the rain. Instinctively, she reached for him. The child smiled, and in that moment, she felt warmth bloom in her chest. The twist? The child was her future self, reborn to thank her for breaking the cycle.
