The Silk Road, a vast network of trade routes connecting East Asia with the Mediterranean, facilitated the exchange of goods, ideas, and cultures between civilizations for over a millennium (from around 2nd century BCE to 14th century CE). While silk was the most iconic commodity, the trade encompassed a diverse array of goods, technologies, and cultural practices. Below is a detailed breakdown:

---

### **Main Goods Traded Along the Silk Road**

1. **Silk**  
   - **Origin**: China (exclusive to Chinese sericulture until the 6th century CE).  
   - **Role**: The namesake of the route, silk was prized for its softness, durability, and luxurious texture. It was traded westward in exchange for precious metals, glass, and horses.  
   - **Impact**: Became a symbol of status in Rome, Persia, and later Europe.

2. **Spices**
