The Silk Road was a vast network of trade and cultural exchange routes connecting ancient China to the Mediterranean. Originating during the Han Dynasty, it facilitated the movement of goods, technology, and ideas such as papermaking and Buddhism, enriching both East and West. Both overland and maritime routes flourished, bringing diverse cultural influences and new products to different regions. The Tang dynasty was its golden age, marked by vibrant cultural exchange. Despite its decline after the Ming, the Silk Road’s legacy remains visible in world heritage sites and cultural traditions.

**Key points:**
1. The Silk Road linked China to the Mediterranean, enabling trade and cultural exchange.
2. It spread innovations, religions, and cultural practices across continents.
3. Both overland and maritime routes flourished, leaving a lasting cultural and historical legacy.
