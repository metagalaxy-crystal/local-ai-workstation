Jane Austen wrote the novel 'Pride and Prejudice.' It was first published in the 19th century, specifically in 1813. The novel is set in England during the Regency era and is renowned for its wit, social commentary, and exploration of themes such as marriage, class, and gender roles.

To provide more context, Jane Austen was a prominent English novelist known for her keen observations of the social dynamics of her time. 'Pride and Prejudice' follows the story of Elizabeth Bennet, one of five sisters, as she navigates the complexities of courtship and societal expectations, ultimately leading to a nuanced exploration of love and personal growth. The novel's enduring popularity can be attributed to its timeless themes and memorable characters, making it a cornerstone of English literature.
