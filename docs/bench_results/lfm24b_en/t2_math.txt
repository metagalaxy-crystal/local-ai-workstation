Let's solve the problem step-by-step.

**Let:**
- \( C \) = number of chickens
- \( R \) = number of rabbits

**Given:**
1. Each chicken and each rabbit has **1 head**.
   \[
   C + R = 35 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
   \]
2. Chickens have **2 legs**, and rabbits have **4 legs**.
   \[
   2C + 4R = 94 \quad \text{(Equation 2)}
   \]

**Step 1: Simplify Equation 2**
Divide the entire equation by 2 to simplify:
\[
C + 2R = 47 \quad \text{(Equation 2a)}
\]

**Step 2: Subtract Equation 1 from Equation 2a**
\[
(C + 2R) - (C + R) = 47 - 35
\]
\[
C + 2R - C - R = 12
\]
\[
R = 12
\]

**Step 3: Find \( C \) using Equation 1**
\[
C + 12 = 35
\]
\[
C = 35 - 12
\]
\[
C = 23
\]

**Final Answer:**
\[
\boxed{23 \text{ chickens and } 12 \text{ rabbits}}
\]

**Verification:**
- **Heads:** \( 23 + 12 = 35 \) ✔️
- **Legs:** \( 2 \times 23 + 4 \times 12 = 46 + 48 = 94 \) ✔️
