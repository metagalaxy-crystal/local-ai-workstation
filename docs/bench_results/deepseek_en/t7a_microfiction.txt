
After her fourteenth rebirth, she decided not to save him again. The scent of ozone and burnt earth had become familiar, clinging to her skin like a second language. This time, it felt different though, more final. She watched from the edge of the clearing, her form shifting momentarily in the fading light – a young woman, this time, with clothes smelling faintly of lavender and wool. He stumbled towards the precipice, his laughter echoing his carelessness. Before she could act, he paused, looked back, and saw her. Not the shadowy figure from last time, or the old hag from the time before that, but a woman with dark, intelligent eyes and a familiarly weary expression. The same expression mirrored in the glassy reflection of the man still reaching for the edge. Her heart constricted. The twist of recognition was sharper than any blade; the man she’d risked everything to save was her own past self, and he always would be.
